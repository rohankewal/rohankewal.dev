I have been posting on LinkedIn for a while. The posts do their job there and
then they disappear, which seems like a waste of something I already wrote.

So from here on, everything goes on this site too. The older LinkedIn posts are
getting back-dated to when they first went out, and anything new lands here at
the same time.

## What to expect

Short pieces about the work: WordPress and Shopify builds, full-stack Svelte and
Node projects, the SEO side of things, and the parts of freelancing that nobody
warns you about. Written when something comes up worth writing down, not on a
schedule.

If you want the posts as they land, there is [an RSS feed](/rss.xml).
