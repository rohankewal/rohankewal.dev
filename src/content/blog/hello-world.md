Hey, I'm Rohan — a full-stack web developer based in New Jersey. I've been writing code since high school, when I swapped pharmacy school dreams for a C++ compiler and never looked back.

## What I Do

These days I work as a Web Developer and Digital Operations Specialist at Streamline Publishing, where I build and maintain sites with Shopify, WordPress, and ClickFunnels. On the side, I do freelance WordPress work for clients like [GP Automate](https://gpautomate.com), a London-based company building software for medical offices.

Before Streamline, I cut my teeth at Citro Digital working with C#, .NET, and the Kentico CMS, and before that at Vaswani Inc. building React and Next.js apps during a two-year internship. I graduated with a Bachelor's in Computer Science in 2021.

## The Setup

I'm a big believer that your tools matter. I use [Neovim](https://neovim.io/) as my editor with the Catppuccin colorscheme, [iTerm2](https://iterm2.com/) as my terminal, and [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) as my font. My daily driver is a Mac Studio with an M1 Max chip, a 34" LG curved monitor, and a Happy Hacking Keyboard. You can see the full breakdown on my [setup page](/setup).

## Outside of Code

When I'm not at my desk, I'm usually at the gym, gaming, or spending time with family. I try not to make my entire personality about code — but I won't pretend it isn't a big part of it.

## Why This Blog

I'm starting this blog fresh. I want a place to write about things I'm learning, projects I'm working on, tools I'm trying out, and the occasional opinion on how things should be built. No filler, no fluff — just honest posts from a working developer.

If any of that sounds interesting, stick around. More posts coming soon.
